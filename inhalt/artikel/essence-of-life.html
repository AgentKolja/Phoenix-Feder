How do I grasp for the essence of life?

With which part of me?

  1. A part that can be in absolute stillness
  2. A part that realises the uniqueness of any given moment and is mindful of everything it offers
  3. A part that realises the beauty and coherence of nature
  4. A part that is compassionate and loving towards oneself and others
  5. A part that is grateful for all the blessings he is gifted
  6. A part that is not concerned by afflictions but frees itself in a resistant manner
  7. A part that transcends the entirety of a feeling, a scene, an image, a taste or a melody
  8. A part that composes a melody of his own with the strings of his voice and the beat of his heart
  9. A part that delves in happy and endless imagination
  10. A curiosity of how things, beings and their nature exist
  11. A part that grasps God in the good of a heart
  12. A part which is faithful to a good future
  13. A part to which I always return and that I never truly leave even after death
  14. A growing part, especially in the face of death
  15. A part that cherishes the most precious human life he owns
